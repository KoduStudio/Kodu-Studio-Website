FAQs

The questions that come up before most projects, answered properly. The short version: most sites take eight to twelve weeks, and you deal with me the whole way through.

  • A single page site is usually three to four weeks. A full site with brand work behind it runs eight to twelve. Most of that is strategy and design; the build is the quick part. I take on a small number of projects at a time, so yours is never sitting in a queue.

  • Most of them are built with Astro and deployed on Cloudflare. Astro ships next to no JavaScript, so pages arrive as plain HTML and start rendering straight away, and Cloudflare serves them from a data centre near whoever is loading the site. The result is a site that feels instant on a phone on mobile data, which is where most of your visitors are. When a project needs a CMS you can edit yourself, I build it in Webflow and apply the same thinking.

  • Every project is quoted as a fixed fee once we have scoped it on a call, so there are no hourly surprises. Cost tracks the size of the site and how much brand and strategy work sits behind it. If there is a budget you need to stay inside, say so early and I will tell you honestly what fits.

  • Time early on, and the truth about your business. A kickoff call, whatever you already have (logos, photography, copy, analytics), and one person who can give feedback and make decisions. If copy or photography is missing, I will tell you before we start rather than halfway through.

  • Yes, if you want. Hosting on Cloudflare is inexpensive, and I can keep it running, make changes as you need them and keep an eye on performance. If you would rather own it outright, I hand the whole thing over with documentation and no lock in.

  • Often, yes. If you have a brand you are happy with, I work inside it rather than reinventing it. If you have a developer or an internal marketing team, I can design and hand over, or build alongside them.
